About

Research with rigor.
Engineering with purpose.

My work sits at the intersection of academic inquiry and applied innovation.

I am a Member of Technical Staff at QuTwo in Helsinki, working on AI and quantum computing systems. Before that, I spent three years as Senior Engineer at Huawei's European Research Institute: I led the OCR project, contributed to the video generation foundation model for Huawei Cloud, and led the autonomous driving project for video and LiDAR data generation on distributed GPU and NPU infrastructure.

My research covers generative models, medical image analysis, facial analysis and biometrics, remote sensing, and Arabic NLP.

I care about systems that do more than perform well in a benchmark: they should remain robust in complex environments and create meaningful real-world value. Open source, reproducibility, and productive collaboration are central to how I work.
