About

Research with rigor.
Engineering with purpose.

My work sits at the intersection of academic inquiry and applied innovation.

I currently serve as a Member of Technical Staff at QutWO. My broader work spans computer vision, facial analysis, biometrics, document understanding, biomedical imaging, and generative models.

I care about systems that do more than perform well in a benchmark: they should remain robust in complex environments and create meaningful real-world value. Open source, reproducibility, and productive collaboration are central to how I work.
